ESP Task 1: Project Planning Lab

Practise the planning task: build a Gantt chart in a spreadsheet, allocate the team, cost the resources, check affordability over three years and write a rationale that explains why. Choose the Digital Infrastructure or Cyber Security scenario. Every organisation, person and price is fictional - this is original practice material built around the task type, not the live assessment.

Fault-Finding Lab

You are handed a broken network. Find and fix the planted faults the way a practical assessment expects: work bottom-up through the OSI model, prove each fault with show commands, ping and ipconfig, fix it, re-test, and record the evidence in your troubleshooting log.

⏱ 00:00
The bottom-up method — what to check at each layer
Layer 1 · PhysicalWhich link light is red or amber? Is the cable in the right port? Is the port shut down? Serial: is there a clock rate?show ip interface brief · show interfaces · show interfaces status · show controllers serial 0/0/0 · show cdp neighbors
Layer 2 · Data linkIs the port in the right VLAN? Is the trunk up, carrying the VLANs, with matching native VLANs? Serial encapsulation the same both ends?show vlan brief · show interfaces trunk · show mac address-table · show interfaces s0/0/0
Layer 3 · NetworkRight IP, mask and gateway? Duplicate address? Does every router have a route there and back?ipconfig /all · ping · tracert · show ip route · show ip protocols · show ip ospf neighbor
Layers 4–7 · Filtering & servicesIs an ACL dropping it? Do DHCP requests get relayed? Does the PC use the right DNS server?show access-lists · show running-config · ipconfig /renew · nslookup · curl

Diagram Lab

A Visio-style diagramming tool for every diagram on your course: flowcharts, data flow diagrams, entity relationship diagrams and network diagrams. Drag shapes from the stencils, join them with connectors that stay glued when you move things, label everything, then let the lab check your diagram against the rules for that diagram type. All organisations, people and addresses are fictional.

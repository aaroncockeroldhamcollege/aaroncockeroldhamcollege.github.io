Network Troubleshooting Lab

You are on the help desk. Users raise tickets describing what they see, not what is wrong. Work each ticket the way a support technician does: read the network documentation, test bottom-up (physical → data link → network → services) with ipconfig, ping, tracert and nslookup, inspect PCs, access points, cables and firewall rules, fix the fault, re-test, and record the evidence in your fault log. Packet Tracer-style training simulator — every organisation, person, name and address is fictional.

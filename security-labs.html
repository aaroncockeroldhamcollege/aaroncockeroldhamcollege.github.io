Rules of engagement

Before you start

Security Labs is a training simulation. Every system here is fictional and nothing you do leaves your browser. By continuing you agree that — here and in real life — you will:

  • Only test systems you own or have written permission to test.
  • Stay within the authorised scope you are given.
  • Never use these techniques to access, damage or disrupt real systems or data.
  • Report weaknesses responsibly and focus on how to fix them.

Unauthorised access to computer systems is a criminal offence under the Computer Misuse Act 1990 (UK) and equivalent laws elsewhere.

Physical Topology Lab

A logical diagram shows what connects to what. A physical topology shows where it really is: which room each device sits in, which cabinet and rack unit, which wall outlet and patch-panel port, and what type and length of cable runs between them. Design it in a Network Notepad-style editor, then let the checker test it against the cabling rules. All organisations and addresses are fictional.

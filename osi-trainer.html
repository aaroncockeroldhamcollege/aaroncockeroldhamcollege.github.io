OSI & TCP/IP Trainer

Learn the seven OSI layers and the four-layer TCP/IP model, then drill them: put the layers in order, drop protocols and PDUs onto the right layer, and test yourself with a quiz. Your best scores are remembered on this device.

The two models

The OSI model has seven layers; TCP/IP collapses them into four. Each OSI layer maps onto a TCP/IP layer.

OSI — 7 layers

Top→bottom: All People Seem To Need Data Processing.
Bottom→top: Please Do Not Throw Sausage Pizza Away.

TCP/IP — 4 layers

Data is encapsulated going down (headers added) and de-encapsulated going up.

PDUs & where things live

The Protocol Data Unit — what the data is called at each layer — plus the kit and protocols you meet there.

Put the OSI layers in order

Drag (or use the ▲▼ buttons) to arrange them top to bottom, Layer 7 at the top. Then check.

Match protocols & PDUs to layers

Drag each item onto the layer it belongs to, then check. Tap a placed chip to send it back.

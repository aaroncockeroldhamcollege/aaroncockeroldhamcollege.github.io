Network Design Lab

Design a network for a client from a written brief, the way project and exam tasks ask you to: draw the topology, plan the IP addressing with VLSM, check the design against the brief, then justify your choices and export a design report.

Guided task

Client briefs

Each client tells you who uses the network, how many devices they have, how much they expect to grow, what they can spend, and what they need for security and availability. Your design must answer every line of the brief. The dental practice is the guided walk-through; the others are independent, graded tasks.

Justify your design

Explain why, not just what. Link each choice back to a line in the brief (users, growth, budget, security, availability). Aim for 40+ words per prompt.

Design report

The report collects your diagram, addressing table, checker results and justification on one page, ready to print or paste into your project write-up.

Packet Analyser Lab

A safe, Wireshark-style packet analyser for a pretend office network. Choose which protocols the network should generate, capture the traffic, filter it like a network technician, open the details of each packet, and export a capture file. Nothing here touches a real network — every packet is simulated in your browser.

Digital Labs

Networking, Systems & Cyber Labs

A suite of safe, self-contained practice labs from Oldham College Digital Labs. Everything runs in your browser — no real networks or systems are ever touched.

⚖️
Practice environment. The networking labs simulate router, switch and firewall command lines; the systems and cyber labs are fictional sandboxes for learning the method and the defences. The tools work only on the invented targets inside each page and never touch real systems.

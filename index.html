Networking & Cyber Labs

A suite of safe, self-contained practice labs for the Cisco Networking Academy at Oldham College. Everything runs in your browser — no real networks or systems are ever touched.

⚖️
Practice environment. The networking labs simulate Cisco IOS; the cyber labs are fictional sandboxes for learning the method and the defences. The tools work only on the invented targets inside each page and never touch real systems.

About this lab

      

        
The lab has two Catalyst 8200L edge routers, two Catalyst 1000 switches, an ASA 5505 firewall, a wireless access point, three PCs, two laptops, a printer, an IP phone and a web server. Select any device on the workspace, or in the bar along the bottom, to open it.

        
It simulates the IOS and ASA command lines, VLANs, trunks (with DTP and native VLAN mismatches), voice VLANs, router-on-a-stick, static routing, DHCP servers and relays, ASA security levels, NAT, ICMP inspection and access lists, WPA2 wireless, ARP, ping, traceroute and web requests.

        
Spanning tree, dynamic routing protocols, call control for the phone and remote SSH or Telnet sessions are not simulated. Their configuration commands are accepted where noted.

        
Your work is kept in this browser and survives a page refresh. Use File, then Save progress, to carry on somewhere else, and Download activity log to hand in a record of your commands and their output. Everything runs in the page; nothing is sent anywhere.

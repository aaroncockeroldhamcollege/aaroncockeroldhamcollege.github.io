About this lab

      

        
The lab has two Catalyst 1000 switches joined by Gi1/0/24, with four PCs (two on each switch) already addressed in 192.168.1.0/24. Select any device on the workspace, or in the bar along the bottom, to open it.

        
It simulates the IOS command line, VLANs, access ports, 802.1Q trunks (with DTP and native VLAN mismatches), ARP and ping. You can configure the switches in the CLI tab or in the Config tab, a Packet Tracer-style form with a VLAN Database page and a page for each port. Both change the same configuration, and the Config tab shows the equivalent IOS commands for everything you click. Assessments may expect the CLI, so learn the commands as well.

        
Spanning tree, dynamic routing protocols, call control for the phone and remote SSH or Telnet sessions are not simulated. Their configuration commands are accepted where noted.

        
Your work is kept in this browser and survives a page refresh. Use File, then Save progress, to carry on somewhere else, and Download activity log to hand in a record of your commands and their output. Everything runs in the page; nothing is sent anywhere.
